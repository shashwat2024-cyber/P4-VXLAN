Project: Performance Evaluation and Tenant Isolation of P4-Based VXLAN

Client command: h1 iperf3 -c 192.168.10.2 -t N

Trial 2 duration: 10 seconds

Latency, packet loss, baseline comparison, and tenant-isolation results
