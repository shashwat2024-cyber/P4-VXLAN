P4 VXLAN SECURITY VERIFICATION
Date: 31 October 2026

1. PURPOSE
Verify that legitimate VXLAN traffic arriving through the underlay interface
is decapsulated and delivered, while VXLAN-shaped traffic injected through
a tenant-facing interface is rejected.

2. LEGITIMATE UNDERLAY VXLAN TEST
Ingress interface: vtep1-eth3 (underlay, port 3)
VNI: 1001
Inner destination: 192.168.10.2
UDP destination port: 9999

Result: One matching packet was captured at h2.
Interpretation: Legitimate underlay VXLAN traffic was delivered successfully.

Evidence: h2_underlay_valid.pcap

3. TENANT-SIDE VXLAN INJECTION TEST
Ingress interface: vtep1-eth1 (tenant-facing, port 1)
VNI: 1001
Inner destination: 192.168.10.2
UDP destination port: 9999

Result: Zero matching packets were captured at h2.
Interpretation: The tenant-originated VXLAN-shaped packet was not delivered
to h2, consistent with the ingress-port decapsulation restriction.

Evidence: h2_tenant_injection.pcap

4. CONCLUSION
The tests provide packet-capture evidence that legitimate VXLAN traffic
arriving on the underlay interface is delivered, while the tested
VXLAN-shaped packet arriving on a tenant-facing interface is not delivered.

This verification covers the tested packet and interfaces. It does not
establish protection against every possible attack or malformed packet.
